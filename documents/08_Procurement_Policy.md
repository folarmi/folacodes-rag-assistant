# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that all purchases made by Folacodes Technologies are transparent, cost-effective, ethical, and aligned with business objectives.

This policy establishes the procedures for requesting, approving, purchasing, receiving, and managing goods and services on behalf of the company.

This policy should be read together with:

- POL-001 Employee Handbook
- POL-002 Code of Conduct
- POL-007 Expense and Travel Policy
- POL-009 Data Privacy Policy

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees who:

- Purchase goods or services
- Select vendors
- Approve purchases
- Manage supplier relationships
- Approve contracts

It covers purchases including:

- Hardware
- Software
- Cloud Services
- Office Equipment
- Professional Services
- Consulting
- Marketing Services
- Training
- Office Supplies

---

# 3. Procurement Principles

Every purchase should:

- Support a legitimate business need.
- Deliver value for money.
- Be fairly evaluated.
- Comply with company policies.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Be appropriately approved.
- Be accurately documented.

---

# 4. Procurement Process

The standard procurement process consists of:

1. Business Need Identified
2. Purchase Request Submitted
3. Budget Verification
4. Vendor Selection
5. Approval
6. Purchase Order (where applicable)
7. Goods or Services Delivered
8. Invoice Review
9. Payment
10. Record Retention

No employee may bypass this process without written approval.

---

# 5. Approval Authority

| Purchase Value   | Required Approval             |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Up to $500       | Line Manager                  |
| $501 – $2,000    | Department Head               |
| $2,001 – $10,000 | Finance Director              |
| Above $10,000    | Chief Executive Officer (CEO) |

Approval thresholds apply to the total purchase value and may not be intentionally divided into smaller purchases to avoid higher approval requirements.

---

# 6. Purchase Requests

Purchase requests should include:

- Business justification
- Required delivery date
- Estimated cost
- Budget code
- Vendor (if known)

Incomplete requests may be returned for clarification.

---

# 7. Vendor Selection

Employees should select vendors based on objective criteria, including:

- Cost
- Quality
- Experience
- Security
- Delivery capability
- Customer support
- Financial stability

Where practical, at least **three competitive quotations** should be obtained for purchases above **$2,000**.

Exceptions require Finance approval.

---

# 8. Preferred Vendors

Folacodes Technologies maintains a list of preferred suppliers for commonly purchased goods and services.

Employees should use preferred vendors whenever practical.

Using non-approved vendors may require additional approval and due diligence.

---

# 9. Software Procurement

All software purchases, including subscriptions, require review by the Information Technology Department.

Software evaluations should consider:

- Business need
- Security
- Licensing
- Integration
- Cost
- Vendor reputation
- Ongoing support

No employee may purchase software using a personal account for company business without prior approval.

---

# 10. Cloud Services

Cloud platforms and SaaS subscriptions require approval from:

- Information Technology
- Finance
- Business Owner

Departments should avoid creating duplicate subscriptions where an existing company solution already satisfies the requirement.

---

# 11. Hardware Purchases

Company hardware should be purchased only through approved procurement channels.

Standard equipment includes:

- Laptop
- Monitor
- Keyboard
- Mouse
- Docking Station
- Headset

Requests for non-standard equipment require business justification.

---

# 12. Emergency Purchases

Emergency purchases may be made where delaying procurement would significantly impact:

- Customer service
- Business continuity
- Information security
- Legal compliance

Emergency purchases must be reported to Finance within **one business day**.

Supporting documentation remains mandatory.

---

# 13. Vendor Due Diligence

Before engaging a new supplier, the company may evaluate:

- Business registration
- Financial stability
- Information security practices
- Data privacy controls
- References
- Insurance
- Compliance history

High-risk vendors may require additional review by Legal or Information Security.

---

# 14. Contracts

Employees may not sign contracts on behalf of Folacodes Technologies unless they have delegated authority.

Contracts should be reviewed by:

- Finance
- Legal (where applicable)
- Information Technology (for technology purchases)
- Information Security (where company data is involved)

---

# 15. Ethical Procurement

Employees must:

- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Disclose relationships with vendors.
- Refuse improper gifts or incentives.
- Treat suppliers fairly.
- Protect confidential supplier information.

Vendor selection must never be influenced by personal relationships.

---

# 16. Purchase Orders

Purchase Orders (POs) should normally be issued before:

- Equipment purchases
- Consulting engagements
- Software procurement
- Major service contracts

Invoices received without an approved Purchase Order may be delayed or rejected.

---

# 17. Receiving Goods

Departments receiving purchased goods should verify:

- Correct quantity
- Correct specification
- No visible damage
- Complete delivery

Discrepancies should be reported to Procurement within **two business days**.

---

# 18. Record Retention

Procurement records including:

- Quotations
- Purchase Orders
- Contracts
- Invoices
- Approval records

must be retained for **seven (7) years** unless a longer period is required by law.

---

# 19. Responsibilities

## Employees

Employees are responsible for:

- Following procurement procedures.
- Providing accurate purchase requests.
- Protecting company funds.
- Reporting procurement concerns.

---

## Managers

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing business justification.
- Confirming budget availability.
- Approving only necessary purchases.

---

## Finance Department

Finance is responsible for:

- Budget verification.
- Procurement oversight.
- Supplier payments.
- Record retention.
- Policy administration.

---

## Information Technology

The IT Department is responsible for reviewing:

- Hardware
- Software
- Cloud Services
- Technology Vendors

to ensure compatibility, security, and supportability.

---

# 20. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I buy software using my personal credit card?

No. Company software purchases should follow the procurement process unless an emergency exception has been approved.

---

### How many quotations are required?

Purchases above **$2,000** should normally include at least **three competitive quotations**.

---

### Can I split a purchase into smaller amounts to avoid approvals?

No. Intentionally dividing purchases to bypass approval thresholds is strictly prohibited.

---

### Who approves a $12,000 purchase?

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

---

### How long are procurement records retained?

Procurement records are retained for **seven years** unless legal requirements specify otherwise.

---

### Can I choose a vendor because my friend owns the company?

No. Employees must disclose conflicts of interest, and vendor selection must be objective.

---

# 21. Questions

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Finance Department.

Technology procurement questions should also be directed to the Information Technology Department.

# Corporate Policy Index

## Purpose

This index provides a central reference to all corporate policies maintained by Folacodes Technologies.

Employees should use this document to identify the policy that governs a particular topic before seeking further guidance from Human Resources, Information Technology, Finance, Legal & Compliance, or their Line Manager.

The latest approved version of each policy is maintained in the company document management system.

# Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to:

- Read and understand policies relevant to their role.
- Follow all applicable company policies.
- Complete required training.
- Ask questions where clarification is needed.
- Report suspected policy violations promptly.

---

# Policy Review

All company policies are reviewed at least annually or sooner where:

- Laws change.
- Regulatory requirements change.
- Business operations change significantly.
- Material risks are identified.
- Executive Management requests an earlier review.

Superseded versions are archived in accordance with the company's records management requirements.

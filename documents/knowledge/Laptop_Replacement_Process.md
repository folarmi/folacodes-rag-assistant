# Laptop Replacement Process

## When Can You Request Replacement?

Examples include:

- Hardware failure
- Lost device
- Stolen device
- End of lifecycle
- Performance issues

---

## Request Process

1. Submit an IT Service Desk request.
2. IT diagnoses the issue.
3. Manager approval may be required.
4. Replacement is scheduled.
5. Data is transferred where possible.

---

## Lost Devices

Lost or stolen laptops must be reported within **24 hours**.

---

## Returning Equipment

Employees should return:

- Laptop
- Charger
- Dock
- Accessories

unless instructed otherwise.

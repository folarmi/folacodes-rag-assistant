# Procurement FAQ

## Can I buy software myself?

No.

Software purchases must follow the Procurement Policy.

---

## How many quotations are required?

Purchases above **$2,000** normally require three quotations.

---

## Who approves purchases?

Approval depends on purchase value.

Refer to the Procurement Policy.

---

## Can I split purchases?

No.

Splitting purchases to avoid approval thresholds is prohibited.

---

## How long does procurement take?

Typical requests are processed within **5–10 business days**, depending on complexity.

---

## Who can sign supplier contracts?

Only employees with delegated authority.

# Expense Submission Guide

## Before Submitting

Ensure that you have:

- Receipts
- Business justification
- Manager approval (where required)

---

## Submission Steps

1. Complete the Expense Claim Form.
2. Attach receipts.
3. Submit through the Finance system.
4. Manager reviews.
5. Finance reviews.
6. Payment is processed.

---

## Deadlines

Expense claims should be submitted within **30 calendar days**.

---

## Reimbursement

Approved claims are generally reimbursed within **10 business days**.

---

## Common Reasons for Rejection

- Missing receipts
- Personal expenses
- Missing approval
- Late submission

# VPN Setup Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how to securely connect to the company VPN.

---

## Requirements

You need:

- Company laptop
- Internet connection
- Company credentials
- MFA device

---

## Steps

1. Open the VPN application.
2. Enter your company email.
3. Enter your password.
4. Approve the MFA notification.
5. Wait until the VPN status shows Connected.

---

## Troubleshooting

### VPN won't connect

- Check your internet.
- Restart the VPN application.
- Verify MFA.
- Restart your computer.

### Authentication failed

Reset your password if necessary.

---

## Important

Always connect to the VPN before accessing:

- SharePoint
- Internal applications
- Development servers
- Customer systems

# Password Reset Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how employees can reset their company password securely.

---

## Before You Begin

Ensure that you have:

- Your company email address
- Your registered Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) device
- Internet access

---

## Resetting Your Password

1. Visit the company password portal.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. Verify your identity using MFA.
4. Create a new password that meets company requirements.
5. Sign in again using your new password.

---

## Password Requirements

Passwords must:

- Be at least 14 characters long
- Include uppercase letters
- Include lowercase letters
- Include numbers
- Include special characters
- Be unique

Passwords must never be reused across company systems.

---

## If You Cannot Access MFA

Contact the IT Service Desk for identity verification before MFA can be reset.

---

## Common Issues

### I forgot my password.

Use the self-service password reset portal.

### My account is locked.

Wait 15 minutes or contact the IT Service Desk.

### I changed my password but cannot log in.

Restart your device and try again. If the issue persists, contact IT.

# Remote Work Checklist

Before starting work each day, ensure you have completed the following:

## Workspace

- ☐ Quiet working environment
- ☐ Stable internet connection
- ☐ Company laptop available
- ☐ Charger connected if needed

---

## Security

- ☐ VPN connected
- ☐ MFA available
- ☐ Screen lock enabled
- ☐ No confidential documents left unattended

---

## Communication

- ☐ Microsoft Teams online
- ☐ Calendar reviewed
- ☐ Daily priorities identified
- ☐ Manager informed of any issues

---

## End of Day

- ☐ Save work to approved company storage
- ☐ Log out of company systems
- ☐ Lock your device
- ☐ Secure any printed documents

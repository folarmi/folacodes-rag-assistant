# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Welcome

Welcome to Folacodes Technologies.

This guide outlines what to expect during your first week.

---

## Day One Checklist

- Meet your Line Manager
- Collect your company laptop
- Activate your email account
- Set up Multi-Factor Authentication
- Review the Employee Handbook
- Complete mandatory HR documentation

---

## First Week

You should:

- Meet your team
- Attend orientation
- Complete security awareness training
- Review company policies
- Gain access to required systems
- Schedule your first one-on-one with your manager

---

## First Month

- Complete role-specific training
- Agree on performance goals
- Meet key stakeholders
- Become familiar with company tools

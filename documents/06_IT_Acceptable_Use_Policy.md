# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that all technology resources owned or managed by Folacodes Technologies are used responsibly, securely, and in a manner that supports business operations.

Technology resources are provided primarily for business purposes and should be used professionally, ethically, and securely.

This policy should be read together with:

- POL-001 Employee Handbook
- POL-002 Code of Conduct
- POL-003 Remote Work Policy
- POL-005 Information Security Policy
- POL-009 Data Privacy Policy

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Interns
- Temporary Staff
- Third-party users granted company system access

The policy covers all company technology resources regardless of whether they are accessed from the office, home, or another approved location.

---

# 3. Technology Resources

Company technology resources include, but are not limited to:

- Laptop computers
- Desktop computers
- Mobile phones
- Tablets
- Monitors
- Corporate email
- Microsoft 365
- Microsoft Teams
- SharePoint
- OneDrive
- VPN
- GitHub Enterprise
- Jira
- Confluence
- Cloud platforms
- Development environments
- Company Wi-Fi
- Internet connectivity

---

# 4. General Principles

Employees are expected to:

- Use company technology responsibly.
- Protect company information.
- Follow security requirements.
- Respect software licensing agreements.
- Report technical problems promptly.

Technology resources remain the property of Folacodes Technologies.

---

# 5. Acceptable Use

Employees may use company technology for legitimate business activities including:

- Software development
- Project management
- Customer communication
- Research
- Documentation
- Collaboration
- Training
- Professional development

Limited personal use is permitted provided it:

- Does not interfere with work.
- Does not violate company policies.
- Does not create security risks.
- Does not consume excessive company resources.
- Does not incur additional costs.

---

# 6. Prohibited Activities

Employees must not use company technology to:

- Download pirated software.
- Access illegal content.
- Conduct personal business.
- Mine cryptocurrency.
- Distribute malware.
- Attempt unauthorized system access.
- Circumvent security controls.
- Share copyrighted material unlawfully.
- Store offensive or inappropriate content.
- Engage in harassment or abusive communications.

Violations may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 7. Internet Usage

Employees should use the internet primarily for business purposes.

Reasonable personal browsing is permitted during breaks provided it:

- Does not affect productivity.
- Does not consume excessive bandwidth.
- Does not expose the company to security risks.

Employees may not intentionally access websites containing:

- Malware
- Illegal content
- Gambling services
- Hate speech
- Pirated media
- Adult content

Access may be restricted by company security systems.

---

# 8. Email Usage

Corporate email should be used professionally.

Employees should:

- Verify recipients before sending confidential information.
- Use professional language.
- Report phishing attempts.
- Avoid opening suspicious attachments.
- Avoid forwarding chain messages or spam.

Corporate email accounts may not be used to register personal online accounts unrelated to work.

---

# 9. Collaboration Tools

Approved collaboration platforms include:

- Microsoft Teams
- Outlook
- SharePoint
- OneDrive
- Jira
- Confluence

Employees should avoid using personal messaging applications for confidential company discussions unless specifically approved.

Meeting recordings should only be created where there is a legitimate business need.

---

# 10. Software Installation

Employees may install software only through approved procedures.

Software requiring administrator privileges must be installed by the IT Department.

Employees may request additional software by submitting a request through the IT Service Desk.

Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.

---

# 11. Open Source Software

Open source software may be used where appropriate.

Employees should:

- Follow applicable software licenses.
- Obtain approval before introducing new dependencies into production systems.
- Ensure open source packages are obtained from trusted sources.

Engineering teams should periodically review dependencies for known security vulnerabilities.

---

# 12. Artificial Intelligence Tools

Approved AI tools may be used for legitimate business purposes such as:

- Drafting documentation
- Code generation
- Brainstorming
- Meeting summaries
- Research

Employees must not submit confidential or restricted information into public AI systems.

Examples include:

- Customer information
- Source code
- Passwords
- Financial information
- API credentials
- Legal agreements

Where enterprise AI platforms are available, employees should use those services instead of public tools.

---

# 13. Removable Media

The use of USB drives and external storage devices should be minimized.

Only encrypted storage devices approved by the IT Department may be used for company information.

Unknown USB devices must never be connected to company computers.

---

# 14. Printing

Employees should avoid printing confidential information unless necessary.

Printed confidential documents should:

- Be collected immediately.
- Be stored securely.
- Be shredded when no longer required.

---

# 15. Monitoring

Employees should understand that company technology resources may be monitored for legitimate business purposes including:

- Security monitoring
- System maintenance
- Compliance investigations
- Performance optimization

Monitoring is conducted in accordance with applicable laws and company policies.

Employees should not expect complete privacy when using company-owned systems.

---

# 16. Personal Devices

Personal devices used for work must comply with POL-005 Information Security Policy.

Employees should not:

- Store restricted company information permanently.
- Share company accounts with family members.
- Disable security controls.

---

# 17. Reporting Issues

Employees should immediately report:

- Lost devices
- Malware infections
- Suspicious emails
- Unauthorized software
- Security concerns
- Hardware failures

Support requests should be submitted through the IT Service Desk.

Urgent security incidents should also be reported to the Information Security Team.

---

# 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I install my own software?

Only software approved through the IT Service Desk may be installed. Software requiring administrator privileges must be installed by IT.

---

### Can I watch YouTube during lunch?

Yes. Reasonable personal internet use during breaks is permitted provided it does not interfere with work or violate company policies.

---

### Can I use ChatGPT?

Yes, for approved business purposes. However, confidential information, customer data, passwords, source code, API keys, and restricted company information must never be entered into public AI tools.

---

### Can I connect my personal USB drive?

Only encrypted USB drives approved by the IT Department may be used for company information.

---

### Can I use my work laptop for online shopping?

Limited personal use is permitted during breaks provided it does not interfere with work, violate company policies, or expose the company to security risks.

---

### Can I install Docker or Visual Studio Code?

Yes, where required for your role and approved through the company's software management process.

---

# 19. Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension of system access
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment

Serious violations involving security breaches may also result in legal action.

---

# 20. Questions

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Information Technology Department.

Security-related questions should be directed to the Information Security Team.

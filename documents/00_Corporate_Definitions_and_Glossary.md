# Corporate Definitions and Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines common terms used throughout Folacodes Technologies' policies. It promotes consistent interpretation of company policies and should be referenced whenever a term is unclear.

This document applies across all company policies.

---

# A

## Access Control

The process of ensuring only authorized individuals can access company systems, applications, facilities, or information.

---

## Annual Leave

Paid leave provided to eligible employees for rest and personal time.

Eligible employees receive **20 working days** of annual leave per calendar year after successfully completing probation.

---

## Approved System

A company-owned or company-authorized application, platform, or service that has been reviewed and approved by the Information Technology Department.

---

# B

## Business Day

Any Monday through Friday excluding officially recognized Nigerian public holidays.

---

## Business Expense

A reasonable and necessary cost incurred while conducting approved company business.

---

## Business Justification

A documented explanation describing why an activity, purchase, or decision is necessary for company operations.

---

# C

## Confidential Information

Business information that is not publicly available and whose unauthorized disclosure could negatively affect Folacodes Technologies.

Examples include:

- Financial reports
- Internal strategies
- Product roadmaps
- Pricing information

---

## Conflict of Interest

A situation where personal interests may improperly influence business decisions.

Employees must disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts.

---

## Contractor

A person or organization providing services under contract but who is not employed directly by the company.

---

# D

## Data Breach

Any unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, or loss of personal or confidential information.

---

## Department Head

The manager responsible for overseeing an entire department and approving higher-level operational decisions.

---

## Development Plan

A documented plan outlining learning objectives, training, certifications, mentoring, or other activities intended to improve employee performance and career growth.

---

# E

## Employee

A person employed by Folacodes Technologies under an employment agreement.

---

## Encryption

The process of converting information into a secure format so that only authorized users can access it.

---

## Expense Claim

A request submitted by an employee seeking reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Expense claims should generally be submitted within **30 calendar days**.

---

# F

## Full-Time Employee

An employee working the company's standard working hours.

Standard working hours are:

Monday to Friday

9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

---

# G

## Goal Setting

The process of agreeing measurable work objectives between an employee and manager at the beginning of a performance cycle.

Goals should follow the SMART principle.

---

# H

## Hybrid Work

A working arrangement combining office-based and remote work.

At Folacodes Technologies employees may work remotely for up to **three days each week** while attending the office for at least **two days**, subject to business requirements.

---

# I

## Incident

Any event that disrupts company operations or threatens information security, privacy, or business continuity.

---

## Information Classification

The categorization of company information according to sensitivity.

Levels include:

- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

---

## Intern

A temporary worker participating in a structured internship programme.

Intern benefits and leave are governed by the Internship Agreement.

---

# L

## Leave Balance

The amount of unused leave available to an employee.

Human Resources maintains the official leave balance.

---

## Line Manager

The employee's direct supervisor responsible for day-to-day management, coaching, leave approvals, and performance discussions.

---

# M

## Manager

Any employee with formal responsibility for supervising one or more employees.

---

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

An authentication method requiring two or more forms of identity verification before granting access.

MFA is mandatory for all company accounts.

---

# O

## One-on-One Meeting

A scheduled meeting between an employee and manager to discuss work progress, performance, priorities, and development.

Managers should conduct one-on-one meetings at least once each month.

---

# P

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

A structured improvement programme designed to help employees whose performance requires improvement.

A PIP typically lasts between **30 and 90 days**.

---

## Personal Data

Any information capable of identifying an individual directly or indirectly.

Examples include:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Employee ID
- Bank details
- IP Address

---

## Preferred Vendor

A supplier approved by the company following procurement evaluation.

Employees should use preferred vendors whenever practical.

---

## Procurement

The process of purchasing goods and services on behalf of the company using approved procedures.

---

## Public Information

Information approved for release outside the organization.

Examples include:

- Company website content
- Marketing materials
- Press releases

---

# R

## Remote Work

Working from a location outside the company's office using approved technology and secure access methods.

Remote workers must connect through the company VPN.

---

## Restricted Information

The highest classification of company information.

Examples include:

- Customer personal data
- Production credentials
- Encryption keys
- Authentication secrets

Restricted information requires the highest level of protection.

---

# S

## Security Incident

Any event that may compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of company information or systems.

Employees should report suspected security incidents within **one hour** of discovery.

---

## Sensitive Personal Data

Personal information requiring additional protection due to its nature.

Examples include:

- Medical records
- Biometric information
- Government-issued identification
- Criminal background checks

---

## SMART Goals

Performance goals that are:

- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable
- Relevant
- Time-bound

---

## Software as a Service (SaaS)

Cloud-hosted software accessed over the internet through subscription rather than local installation.

---

# T

## Third-Party Vendor

An external organization supplying products or services to Folacodes Technologies.

Third-party vendors may undergo due diligence before engagement.

---

# V

## Virtual Private Network (VPN)

A secure encrypted connection used to access company resources remotely.

VPN usage is mandatory for remote access.

---

# W

## Working Hours

The company's standard working hours are:

Monday to Friday

9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Alternative schedules may be approved where business needs require.

---

## Workstation

Any company-approved desktop, laptop, or virtual computing environment used to perform work.

# Expense and Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the rules governing business expenses, employee reimbursements, corporate travel, and entertainment expenses.

The objective is to ensure that company funds are used responsibly, transparently, and consistently.

This policy should be read together with:

- POL-001 Employee Handbook
- POL-003 Remote Work Policy
- POL-008 Procurement Policy

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Managers
- Executives
- Contractors where their agreement specifically permits reimbursement

Personal expenses are never reimbursable unless expressly stated in this policy.

---

# 3. General Principles

Business expenses should be:

- Necessary
- Reasonable
- Cost-effective
- Supported by receipts
- Approved before being incurred where required

Employees are expected to spend company funds as carefully as they would spend their own.

---

# 4. Expense Categories

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved expenses including:

- Business Travel
- Accommodation
- Meals
- Ground Transportation
- Client Entertainment
- Parking
- Toll Fees
- Conference Registration
- Professional Memberships
- Business Communication Costs

---

# 5. Approval Matrix

| Expense Amount | Approval Required                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Up to $500     | Line Manager                       |
| $501 – $2,000  | Department Head                    |
| Above $2,000   | Department Head + Finance Director |

Approval must be obtained before the expense is incurred unless emergency circumstances apply.

---

# 6. Business Travel

Business travel should only occur where virtual collaboration is not practical.

Travel may be approved for:

- Customer meetings
- Project delivery
- Conferences
- Training
- Sales engagements
- Company retreats

Employees should always select the most economical reasonable option.

---

# 7. Travel Booking

All business travel must be booked through the company's approved travel coordinator or approved booking platform.

Employees should not independently purchase flights or hotels without prior approval unless specifically authorized.

Travel requests should be submitted at least **14 calendar days** before departure whenever possible.

---

# 8. Air Travel

The following booking standards apply.

| Flight Duration   | Cabin Class                           |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Less than 5 hours | Economy Class                         |
| 5 hours or longer | Premium Economy (subject to approval) |
| Business Class    | Executive Leadership approval only    |

Employees should select the lowest logical airfare available.

---

# 9. Accommodation

Hotel bookings should be:

- Safe
- Reasonably priced
- Convenient to the business location

Maximum nightly hotel rates should not ordinarily exceed:

| Location      | Maximum Rate |
| ------------- | -----------: |
| Nigeria       |         $180 |
| International |         $300 |

Higher rates require Finance approval.

---

# 10. Meals

Employees travelling overnight may claim reasonable meal expenses.

Maximum daily meal allowance:

| Meal      | Maximum |
| --------- | ------: |
| Breakfast |     $20 |
| Lunch     |     $30 |
| Dinner    |     $50 |

Alcohol is generally not reimbursable unless part of an approved client entertainment event.

---

# 11. Local Transportation

Employees may claim:

- Taxi fares
- Ride-sharing services
- Airport transfers
- Public transportation
- Parking fees
- Toll charges

Luxury transportation should be avoided unless justified by safety or customer requirements.

---

# 12. Mileage Reimbursement

Where employees use their personal vehicle for approved business travel, mileage may be reimbursed.

Mileage reimbursement rate:

**$0.50 per mile**

Mileage claims must include:

- Date
- Destination
- Business purpose
- Distance travelled

---

# 13. Client Entertainment

Reasonable entertainment expenses may be reimbursed where there is a legitimate business purpose.

Examples include:

- Business lunches
- Customer dinners
- Industry networking events

Employees should document:

- Attendees
- Business purpose
- Date
- Location

Entertainment that is excessive or unrelated to business will not be reimbursed.

---

# 14. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are not reimbursable:

- Traffic fines
- Parking tickets
- Personal shopping
- Personal hotel upgrades
- Mini-bar charges
- Personal entertainment
- Family travel expenses
- Passport renewals
- Travel insurance purchased without approval

---

# 15. Receipt Requirements

Employees must retain receipts for all expenses above **$25**.

Receipts should clearly show:

- Merchant
- Date
- Amount
- Currency
- Items purchased

Digital receipts are acceptable.

---

# 16. Submission Deadlines

Expense claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred.

Late submissions may be rejected unless exceptional circumstances exist.

---

# 17. Corporate Credit Cards

Employees issued corporate credit cards must:

- Use them only for approved business expenses.
- Submit supporting receipts promptly.
- Never lend the card to another person.

Lost cards should be reported immediately to both the issuing bank and Finance.

---

# 18. Cash Advances

Employees may request travel cash advances for approved business travel.

Unused funds must be returned within **five business days** after travel concludes.

Supporting receipts remain mandatory.

---

# 19. International Travel

International travel requires approval from:

- Line Manager
- Department Head
- Finance

Where required, Human Resources and Information Security may also review travel requests to assess immigration, tax, and security considerations.

Employees travelling internationally should complete any required travel risk briefings before departure.

---

# 20. Responsibilities

## Employees

Employees are responsible for:

- Obtaining approvals.
- Keeping receipts.
- Submitting claims on time.
- Providing accurate information.
- Spending responsibly.

---

## Managers

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing claims.
- Ensuring business justification.
- Preventing unnecessary expenditure.
- Approving only reasonable expenses.

---

## Finance Department

Finance is responsible for:

- Reviewing claims.
- Processing reimbursements.
- Maintaining expense records.
- Auditing compliance.
- Updating reimbursement guidelines.

---

# 21. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I book my own hotel?

Only if specifically authorized. Otherwise, bookings should be made through the company's approved travel process.

---

### What happens if I lose my receipt?

Finance may request alternative evidence of payment. Reimbursement is not guaranteed without adequate supporting documentation.

---

### Can I upgrade my flight?

Employees may personally pay the additional cost of an upgrade, but the company reimburses only the approved fare.

---

### When will I receive reimbursement?

Approved expense claims are generally reimbursed within **10 business days** after Finance completes its review.

---

### Can I claim meals while working from home?

No. Daily meals during normal remote work are considered personal living expenses and are not reimbursable.

---

### Can I claim tips?

Reasonable gratuities related to approved business travel may be reimbursed where customary.

---

# 22. Questions

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Finance Department.

# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the guidelines governing remote and hybrid work arrangements at Folacodes Technologies.

The company recognizes that flexible work arrangements improve employee wellbeing and productivity while supporting business continuity. Remote work is a privilege and may be modified or withdrawn based on operational needs.

This policy should be read together with:

- POL-001 Employee Handbook
- POL-002 Code of Conduct
- POL-005 Information Security Policy
- POL-006 IT Acceptable Use Policy
- POL-009 Data Privacy Policy

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Full-time employees
- Part-time employees where approved
- Contract employees where specified in their agreement

Interns are generally expected to work on-site unless otherwise approved by Human Resources.

---

# 3. Remote Work Eligibility

Remote work eligibility depends on:

- Job responsibilities
- Team requirements
- Performance history
- Security considerations
- Customer commitments

Not every role is suitable for remote work.

Managers determine eligibility in consultation with Human Resources.

---

# 4. Hybrid Work Arrangement

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **three (3) days per week**.

Employees are expected to work from the office for at least **two (2) working days each week**, unless an alternative arrangement has been approved.

Departments may require additional office attendance for:

- Team planning sessions
- Client meetings
- Training
- Company events
- Performance discussions

---

# 5. Working Hours

Remote employees must remain available during the company's core working hours:

**Monday – Friday**

**9:00 AM – 5:00 PM**

Employees are expected to:

- Attend scheduled meetings.
- Respond to business communications promptly.
- Update their availability status.
- Inform their manager when temporarily unavailable.

Flexible schedules may be approved where business requirements permit.

---

# 6. Remote Workspace Requirements

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable work environment.

The remote workspace should:

- Be quiet and free from unnecessary distractions.
- Have reliable internet access.
- Provide appropriate lighting.
- Support safe and ergonomic working conditions.
- Protect confidential information from unauthorized viewing.

Working from public areas where confidential information may be exposed should be avoided.

---

# 7. Company Equipment

Folacodes Technologies may provide:

- Laptop computer
- Monitor
- Keyboard
- Mouse
- Headset
- Security access tokens

Equipment remains company property.

Employees are responsible for protecting issued equipment from loss, theft, or damage.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Department within **24 hours**.

---

# 8. Internet and Connectivity

Employees are responsible for maintaining reliable internet access capable of supporting:

- Video conferencing
- Cloud applications
- Secure VPN connectivity

Temporary internet outages should be reported to the employee's manager as soon as possible.

Where prolonged outages occur, employees may be required to work from the office or another approved company location.

---

# 9. Information Security

Remote employees must comply with all security requirements contained in:

- POL-005 Information Security Policy
- POL-006 IT Acceptable Use Policy

Employees must:

- Connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Lock devices whenever unattended.
- Prevent family members or visitors from using company devices.
- Report suspected security incidents immediately.

Sensitive company information must never be stored on personal devices unless explicitly approved.

---

# 10. Communication Expectations

Remote employees are expected to remain engaged with their teams.

Employees should:

- Attend scheduled meetings.
- Respond to internal messages within one business hour during working hours.
- Keep calendars up to date.
- Notify colleagues when unavailable.

Video should be enabled during formal meetings unless technical limitations or accessibility needs prevent this.

---

# 11. Performance Expectations

Remote employees are evaluated using the same standards as office-based employees.

Performance is measured by:

- Quality of work
- Timely delivery
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Customer satisfaction
- Achievement of agreed objectives

Remote work does not reduce performance expectations.

Performance reviews continue to follow POL-010 Performance Management Policy.

---

# 12. Expenses

Folacodes Technologies does not reimburse ordinary home utility expenses including:

- Electricity
- Water
- Rent
- Home internet subscriptions

Business-related travel required during remote work may be reimbursed in accordance with POL-007 Expense and Travel Policy.

Any request for home office equipment beyond the standard allocation requires prior approval from the employee's manager and the Finance Department.

---

# 13. International Remote Work

Employees may not perform work from another country without prior written approval.

Approval requires consultation with:

- Human Resources
- Finance
- Information Security

International remote work requests should be submitted at least **20 business days** before the proposed travel date.

Approval depends on:

- Employment regulations
- Tax implications
- Data protection requirements
- Customer obligations

---

# 14. Temporary Remote Work Exceptions

Employees experiencing exceptional circumstances such as:

- Medical recovery
- Family emergencies
- Severe weather
- Transportation disruptions

may request temporary remote work arrangements.

Requests should be submitted to the employee's manager as early as reasonably possible.

Approval is not automatic.

---

# 15. Health and Safety

Employees remain responsible for maintaining a safe working environment while working remotely.

Employees should:

- Use ergonomic seating where possible.
- Take regular screen breaks.
- Report work-related injuries occurring during working hours.
- Follow company health and safety guidance.

---

# 16. Policy Violations

Examples of policy violations include:

- Sharing company devices with unauthorized persons.
- Working remotely from unapproved international locations.
- Repeated failure to attend required meetings.
- Disabling security software.
- Repeated failure to maintain communication during working hours.

Violations may result in disciplinary action under POL-002 Code of Conduct.

---

# 17. Questions

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to Human Resources.

Technical questions relating to remote access should be directed to the IT Department.

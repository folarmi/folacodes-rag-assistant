# Performance Management Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a fair, transparent, and consistent approach to managing employee performance, supporting career growth, recognizing high performance, and addressing performance concerns.

Performance management at Folacodes Technologies is a continuous process rather than a once-a-year event.

This policy should be read together with:

- POL-001 Employee Handbook
- POL-002 Code of Conduct
- POL-004 Leave and Time Off Policy

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Full-time employees
- Part-time employees
- Managers
- Team Leads

Contractors and interns may participate in simplified performance reviews where appropriate.

---

# 3. Performance Management Objectives

The performance management process aims to:

- Align individual goals with company objectives.
- Encourage continuous feedback.
- Recognize outstanding performance.
- Identify development opportunities.
- Support employee career progression.
- Address performance concerns fairly and consistently.

---

# 4. Performance Cycle

Formal performance reviews are conducted **twice each year**.

The cycle consists of:

| Period   | Activity                      |
| -------- | ----------------------------- |
| January  | Goal Setting                  |
| June     | Mid-Year Performance Review   |
| July     | Goal Adjustment (if required) |
| December | Annual Performance Review     |

Managers and employees are encouraged to discuss performance throughout the year rather than waiting for formal review periods.

---

# 5. Goal Setting

At the beginning of each performance cycle, managers and employees should agree on measurable performance goals.

Goals should be:

- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable
- Relevant
- Time-bound (SMART)

Goals may include:

- Project delivery
- Customer satisfaction
- Quality improvements
- Learning objectives
- Innovation
- Leadership contributions
- Team collaboration

---

# 6. Continuous Feedback

Managers should provide regular feedback through:

- One-on-one meetings
- Project reviews
- Coaching sessions
- Informal discussions

Employees are encouraged to seek feedback proactively and discuss challenges early.

Recommended frequency for one-on-one meetings:

**At least once each month.**

---

# 7. Performance Ratings

Performance is assessed using the following rating scale:

| Rating | Description          |
| ------ | -------------------- |
| 5      | Exceptional          |
| 4      | Exceeds Expectations |
| 3      | Meets Expectations   |
| 2      | Needs Improvement    |
| 1      | Unsatisfactory       |

Ratings should be supported by documented examples and evidence.

---

# 8. Performance Evaluation Criteria

Performance may be assessed based on:

- Achievement of goals
- Quality of work
- Technical competence
- Customer focus
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Innovation
- Accountability
- Leadership (where applicable)
- Compliance with company policies

No single criterion should determine an overall performance rating.

---

# 9. Employee Development

Following each formal review, managers and employees should agree on a development plan.

Development activities may include:

- Professional certifications
- Internal training
- Mentoring
- Coaching
- Conferences
- Stretch assignments
- Cross-functional projects

Managers should support employees in identifying appropriate development opportunities.

---

# 10. Promotions

Promotion decisions are based on multiple factors, including:

- Sustained performance
- Business needs
- Demonstrated competencies
- Leadership potential
- Availability of suitable roles

Promotion is not based solely on length of service.

Meeting performance expectations does not automatically guarantee promotion.

---

# 11. Salary Reviews

Salary reviews may be considered following the annual performance review process.

Factors considered include:

- Individual performance
- Market competitiveness
- Internal pay equity
- Company financial performance
- Budget availability

Salary adjustments are not automatic.

---

# 12. Recognition

Managers are encouraged to recognize employee achievements through:

- Public recognition
- Spot awards
- Professional development opportunities
- Additional responsibilities
- Performance bonuses (where applicable)

Recognition should be timely, fair, and aligned with company values.

---

# 13. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

Employees whose performance is consistently below expectations may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

A PIP typically includes:

- Performance concerns
- Expected improvements
- Measurable objectives
- Support provided
- Review dates
- Completion timeline

The standard PIP duration is **30 to 90 days**, depending on the nature of the performance concerns.

A PIP is intended to help employees improve and should not be viewed as automatic preparation for termination.

---

# 14. Unsatisfactory Performance

Where performance does not improve despite reasonable support and a completed PIP, further action may be considered in accordance with company disciplinary procedures and applicable employment laws.

Any decision will take into account:

- Performance history
- Evidence collected
- Support provided
- Employee response
- Business circumstances

---

# 15. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to:

- Participate actively in reviews.
- Complete agreed objectives.
- Seek feedback.
- Raise concerns early.
- Complete agreed development activities.
- Demonstrate company values.

---

# 16. Manager Responsibilities

Managers are expected to:

- Set clear expectations.
- Conduct timely reviews.
- Provide regular feedback.
- Document performance discussions.
- Support employee development.
- Apply ratings consistently and fairly.

---

# 17. Human Resources Responsibilities

Human Resources is responsible for:

- Maintaining the performance management framework.
- Supporting managers.
- Providing training.
- Monitoring consistency.
- Maintaining review records.
- Advising on performance-related matters.

---

# 18. Appeals

Employees who disagree with a performance rating may request a review.

Appeals should:

- Be submitted within **10 business days** of receiving the review outcome.
- Clearly explain the reason for the appeal.
- Include any relevant supporting information.

Appeals are reviewed by the employee's next-level manager together with Human Resources.

The outcome of the appeal will be communicated in writing.

---

# 19. Frequently Asked Questions

### How often are performance reviews conducted?

Formal reviews are conducted twice each year, with continuous feedback encouraged throughout the year.

---

### Are salary increases guaranteed after a good review?

No. Salary adjustments depend on performance, market conditions, company performance, internal equity, and available budget.

---

### Does meeting expectations guarantee promotion?

No. Promotions are based on sustained performance, competencies, business needs, leadership potential, and role availability.

---

### What happens if my performance is below expectations?

Your manager will work with you to identify improvement opportunities. Where necessary, a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be implemented.

---

### How long does a Performance Improvement Plan last?

A PIP typically lasts between **30 and 90 days**, depending on the circumstances.

---

### Can I appeal my performance rating?

Yes. Appeals should be submitted within **10 business days** of receiving your review outcome.

---

# 20. Policy Exceptions

Any exception to this policy must:

- Be documented.
- Include a clear business justification.
- Be approved by Human Resources.

Exceptions will be reviewed periodically to ensure they remain appropriate.

---

# 21. Questions

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Human Resources Department.

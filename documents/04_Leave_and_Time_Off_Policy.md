# Leave and Time Off Policy

## 1. Purpose

Folacodes Technologies recognizes the importance of work-life balance, employee wellbeing, and adequate time away from work. This policy establishes the company's leave entitlements, approval processes, employee responsibilities, and manager responsibilities.

This policy should be read together with:

- POL-001 Employee Handbook
- POL-002 Code of Conduct
- POL-003 Remote Work Policy

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Full-Time Employees
- Part-Time Employees (where contractually eligible)
- Contract Employees where specified in their employment agreement

Intern leave benefits are determined by the Internship Programme Agreement.

---

# 3. Leave Principles

The company encourages employees to:

- Take leave regularly.
- Plan leave well in advance.
- Ensure business continuity.
- Minimize disruption to customers and project delivery.

Leave is not automatically approved simply because an employee has sufficient leave balance.

Business needs are always considered.

---

---

# 5. Annual Leave

Annual Leave is intended to allow employees adequate rest and recovery.

Employees receive **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees become eligible to use annual leave after successfully completing their probation period.

Human Resources maintains official leave balances.

---

## 5.1 Carrying Leave Forward

Employees may carry forward a maximum of **5 unused working days** into the following calendar year.

Carried-forward leave must be used no later than **March 31** of the following year.

Unused carried-forward leave expires after this date unless an exception is approved by Human Resources.

---

## 5.2 Cash Payment Instead of Leave

Annual leave exists to support employee wellbeing.

Employees may not request payment instead of taking annual leave except:

- Upon resignation
- Upon termination of employment
- Where required by applicable employment laws

---

# 6. Leave Approval Process

Annual leave requests should be submitted through the company's HR system at least **10 business days** before the intended leave start date.

Approval requires:

1. Employee Submission
2. Line Manager Approval
3. HR Recording

Leave is not considered approved until confirmation is received through the HR system.

Employees should not make travel arrangements before leave approval.

---

# 7. Peak Business Periods

Certain periods may be designated as critical operational periods.

During these periods:

- Leave requests may be restricted.
- Teams may operate with reduced approval capacity.
- Managers may defer leave where business continuity would be significantly affected.

Human Resources will communicate such periods in advance whenever reasonably possible.

---

# 8. Team Leave Limits

To ensure adequate business coverage:

No more than **30% of employees within the same department** should ordinarily be on annual leave at the same time.

Managers may approve exceptions where business operations permit.

---

# 9. Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 working days** of paid sick leave each calendar year.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and no later than one hour before the start of their workday.

Employees absent due to illness for **more than two consecutive working days** must provide a medical certificate issued by a licensed healthcare provider.

The company reserves the right to request medical documentation where patterns of excessive absenteeism are identified.

---

# 10. Sick Leave During Annual Leave

If an employee becomes seriously ill while on approved annual leave and provides appropriate medical evidence, Human Resources may convert the affected leave days into sick leave.

Approval is subject to verification of supporting documentation.

---

# 11. Compassionate Leave

Employees may receive up to **5 working days** of paid compassionate leave following the death or serious illness of an immediate family member.

Immediate family includes:

- Spouse
- Parent
- Child
- Sibling
- Legal Guardian

Additional unpaid leave may be considered based on individual circumstances.

---

# 12. Maternity Leave

Eligible employees receive **16 weeks** of paid maternity leave.

Employees should notify Human Resources at least **30 calendar days** before their intended leave date where reasonably practicable.

Supporting medical documentation may be requested.

---

# 13. Paternity Leave

Eligible employees receive **10 working days** of paid paternity leave.

The leave should ordinarily be taken within **three months** of the child's birth or adoption.

---

# 14. Study Leave

Employees pursuing approved professional certifications or educational programmes may apply for up to **5 working days** of paid study leave each calendar year.

Approval depends on:

- Relevance to the employee's role.
- Business needs.
- Manager recommendation.

Examination timetables or supporting documentation may be requested.

---

# 15. Unpaid Leave

Employees may request unpaid leave for exceptional personal circumstances.

Approval is entirely discretionary.

Factors considered include:

- Business impact
- Employee performance
- Leave history
- Operational requirements

Unpaid leave does not automatically extend other employment benefits.

---

# 16. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to officially recognized Nigerian public holidays.

Where business operations require employees to work on a public holiday, alternative arrangements such as time off in lieu or additional compensation may be provided in accordance with applicable laws and company practice.

---

# 17. Leave Cancellation

Employees wishing to cancel approved leave should notify their manager immediately.

Managers may approve cancellation provided:

- Business arrangements can reasonably be reversed.
- No significant operational disruption occurs.

The company is not responsible for personal costs arising from cancelled travel arrangements.

---

# 18. Responsibilities

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Planning leave early.
- Providing accurate information.
- Monitoring leave balances.
- Returning to work as scheduled.
- Informing managers of unexpected changes.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing requests fairly.
- Balancing operational needs.
- Responding promptly.
- Maintaining adequate team coverage.
- Escalating unusual cases to Human Resources.

---

# 19. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I take annual leave during probation?

No. Annual leave becomes available after successful completion of the six-month probation period unless otherwise approved by Human Resources.

---

### Can my manager reject my leave request?

Yes. Leave may be declined or rescheduled where operational requirements require business coverage.

---

### Can I work while on annual leave?

Employees are expected to disconnect from work during approved annual leave unless exceptional business circumstances arise.

---

### Can unused leave accumulate indefinitely?

No. Only five working days may be carried forward, and these expire on March 31 of the following year if unused.

---

### Can two members of the same project team take leave simultaneously?

Possibly. Approval depends on business continuity and adequate team coverage.

---

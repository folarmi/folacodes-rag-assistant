# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Code of Conduct is to establish the standards of ethical and professional behaviour expected of every employee, contractor, intern, consultant, and temporary worker at Folacodes Technologies.

Maintaining these standards protects our employees, customers, partners, and reputation.

This policy should be read together with:

- POL-001 Employee Handbook
- POL-005 Information Security Policy
- POL-006 IT Acceptable Use Policy
- POL-009 Data Privacy Policy

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Full-time employees
- Part-time employees
- Contract employees
- Interns
- Consultants working on behalf of Folacodes Technologies

Compliance with this policy is a condition of employment.

---

# 3. Our Values

Every employee is expected to demonstrate the following values:

- Integrity
- Accountability
- Respect
- Innovation
- Collaboration
- Customer Focus
- Continuous Learning

Employees should make decisions that reflect these values even when no specific rule exists.

---

# 4. Professional Behaviour

Employees must:

- Treat everyone with dignity and respect.
- Be courteous in all communications.
- Maintain professionalism in meetings.
- Honour commitments and deadlines.
- Represent the company responsibly.

Employees must avoid behaviour that damages teamwork or the company's reputation.

---

# 5. Equal Opportunity and Inclusion

Folacodes Technologies is committed to providing a workplace free from discrimination.

Employment decisions shall never be based on:

- Age
- Race
- Ethnicity
- Nationality
- Gender
- Religion
- Disability
- Marital Status
- Sexual Orientation

Employees are expected to contribute to an inclusive workplace where everyone feels respected and valued.

---

# 6. Workplace Harassment

Harassment of any kind is prohibited.

Examples include:

- Bullying
- Intimidation
- Threats
- Offensive jokes
- Unwanted physical contact
- Sexual harassment
- Discriminatory remarks

Employees experiencing or witnessing harassment should report the incident to Human Resources immediately.

Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

---

# 7. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests.

Examples include:

- Awarding contracts to businesses owned by close family members.
- Accepting gifts intended to influence business decisions.
- Working for a competitor without approval.
- Using company resources for personal business.

Employees must disclose potential conflicts to Human Resources.

---

# 8. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept modest business gifts valued at **no more than $50** without approval.

Any gift exceeding **$50** must be reported to the employee's manager and Human Resources.

Employees must never:

- Request gifts from vendors.
- Accept cash gifts.
- Accept gifts intended to influence business decisions.

Business meals and promotional items of nominal value are generally acceptable.

---

# 9. Confidential Information

Employees must protect confidential company information.

Examples include:

- Customer information
- Employee records
- Financial information
- Product roadmaps
- Source code
- Security procedures
- Pricing information

Confidential information must never be shared with unauthorized persons.

Additional requirements are provided in POL-005 Information Security Policy and POL-009 Data Privacy Policy.

---

# 10. Company Property

Employees must use company property responsibly.

Company property includes:

- Laptops
- Mobile phones
- Software licences
- Access cards
- Development environments
- Cloud resources

Employees may not intentionally damage, misuse, or remove company property without authorization.

Lost or stolen property must be reported within **24 hours**.

---

# 11. Use of Company Resources

Company resources are provided primarily for business purposes.

Limited personal use is permitted provided it:

- Does not interfere with work.
- Does not violate company policies.
- Does not create security risks.
- Does not incur unreasonable costs.

Employees must comply with POL-006 IT Acceptable Use Policy.

---

# 12. Social Media

Employees should exercise good judgment when using social media.

Employees may not:

- Reveal confidential information.
- Speak on behalf of the company without authorization.
- Publish offensive content that could damage the company's reputation.
- Misrepresent company products or services.

Employees should make it clear when personal opinions are their own.

---

# 13. Workplace Safety

Employees are expected to contribute to a safe workplace.

Employees should:

- Report hazards immediately.
- Follow emergency procedures.
- Use equipment safely.
- Cooperate during emergency drills.

Unsafe behaviour may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 14. Reporting Concerns

Employees are encouraged to report:

- Fraud
- Theft
- Policy violations
- Harassment
- Security incidents
- Ethical concerns

Reports may be made to:

- Immediate Manager
- Human Resources
- Information Security Team (where applicable)

Reports made in good faith are protected from retaliation.

---

# 15. Disciplinary Process

Violations of this policy may result in one or more of the following actions:

1. Verbal Warning
2. Written Warning
3. Final Written Warning
4. Suspension
5. Termination of Employment

The disciplinary action taken depends on:

- Severity of the violation
- Previous disciplinary history
- Business impact
- Legal requirements

Certain serious offences may result in immediate termination.

Examples include:

- Theft
- Fraud
- Violence
- Deliberate disclosure of confidential information
- Serious security breaches

---

# 16. Employee Responsibilities

Every employee is responsible for:

- Reading company policies.
- Completing mandatory compliance training.
- Reporting suspected violations.
- Cooperating with investigations.
- Acting honestly and ethically.

Failure to understand a policy does not exempt an employee from complying with it.

---

# 17. Questions

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to Human Resources.

Employees should always refer to the latest approved version of this document.

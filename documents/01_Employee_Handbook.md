# Employee Handbook

## 1. Purpose

Welcome to **Folacodes Technologies**.

This Employee Handbook introduces employees to the company's culture, employment practices, workplace expectations, benefits, and policies. It serves as the primary reference guide for all employees and should be read together with the company's other policy documents.

This handbook applies to all permanent, contract, part-time, and intern employees unless otherwise stated.

---

## 2. About Folacodes Technologies

Folacodes Technologies is a software engineering and digital transformation company headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria.

We specialize in:

- Custom Software Development
- Artificial Intelligence Solutions
- Cloud Engineering
- UI/UX Design
- Product Management
- IT Consulting
- Business Process Automation

Our mission is to build secure, scalable, and innovative technology solutions that solve real-world business problems.

Our core values are:

- Integrity
- Innovation
- Customer Success
- Collaboration
- Continuous Learning
- Accountability

# 4. Employment Categories

Folacodes Technologies maintains four categories of employment.

## 4.1 Full-Time Employees

Employees working a standard schedule of 40 hours per week and eligible for company benefits.

## 4.2 Part-Time Employees

Employees working fewer than 40 hours weekly. Benefits are determined by employment agreements.

## 4.3 Contract Employees

Individuals engaged for a defined project or fixed duration under a contractual agreement.

## 4.4 Interns

Students or graduates participating in internship or graduate trainee programmes.

---

# 5. Probation Period

Every newly hired employee serves a probation period of **six (6) months**.

During probation:

- Managers conduct monthly performance reviews.
- Employees receive coaching and feedback.
- Employment may be terminated in accordance with applicable labour laws.
- Employees are expected to comply with all company policies.

Successful completion of probation is confirmed in writing by Human Resources.

---

# 6. Working Hours

The company's standard working hours are:

**Monday – Friday**

**9:00 AM – 5:00 PM**

Employees are expected to:

- Arrive on time.
- Attend scheduled meetings.
- Be available during core working hours.
- Record working time where required.
- Notify their manager if delayed.

Flexible schedules may be approved based on operational needs.

Hybrid work arrangements are governed by **POL-003 Remote Work Policy**.

---

# 7. Attendance

Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance.

If unable to report for work because of illness or emergency, employees must notify their manager no later than **one hour before** the beginning of their workday.

Repeated unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 8. Remote Work

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **three (3) days each week**, subject to:

- Manager approval
- Business requirements
- Compliance with company security policies

Employees working remotely must comply with:

- POL-003 Remote Work Policy
- POL-005 Information Security Policy
- POL-006 IT Acceptable Use Policy

---

# 9. Compensation

Employees are paid monthly through direct bank transfer.

Payroll is processed on the **last working day of every month**.

Employees are responsible for ensuring their banking information remains accurate.

Payroll enquiries should be directed to Human Resources.

---

# 10. Employee Benefits

Eligible employees may receive:

- Wellness Programmes
- Paid Annual Leave
- Paid Sick Leave
- Public Holiday Leave

# 12. Learning and Development

Folacodes Technologies encourages continuous professional development.

Employees may request sponsorship for:

- Professional Certifications
- Technical Courses
- Conferences
- Workshops
- Industry Events

Training requests require approval from the employee's manager before registration.

---

# 13. Company Property

Employees may receive company assets including:

- Laptop Computer
- Monitor
- Mobile Phone
- Security Access Card
- Software Licenses
- Accessories

Employees are responsible for protecting company property against:

- Theft
- Loss
- Damage
- Misuse

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Department within **24 hours**.

---

# 14. Information Security

All employees share responsibility for protecting company information.

Employees must:

- Use strong passwords.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Lock computers when unattended.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Report suspected security incidents immediately.

Additional security requirements are described in **POL-005 Information Security Policy**.

---

# 15. Acceptable Technology Use

Company devices, software, internet access, and networks are provided primarily for business purposes.

Employees must comply with **POL-006 IT Acceptable Use Policy**.

Employees may not:

- Install unauthorised software.
- Share company passwords.
- Disable security software.
- Access illegal or inappropriate content using company devices.

---

# 16. Business Expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business expenses.

To qualify:

- Expenses must be pre-approved where required.
- Original receipts must be submitted within **30 calendar days**.
- Claims must comply with **POL-007 Expense and Travel Policy**.

---

# 17. Performance Reviews

Employee performance reviews are conducted **twice each year**.

Performance evaluations consider:

- Goal Achievement
- Technical Competence
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Professional Conduct
- Continuous Learning

The complete review process is documented in **POL-010 Performance Management Policy**.

---

# 18. Workplace Conduct

Employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues respectfully.
- Maintain professional behaviour.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Follow company policies.
- Report misconduct appropriately.

Violations may result in disciplinary action under **POL-002 Code of Conduct**.

---

# 19. Policy Compliance

All employees are responsible for understanding and complying with company policies.

Failure to comply may result in:

- Verbal Warning
- Written Warning
- Suspension
- Termination of Employment

depending on the seriousness of the violation.

---

# 20. Questions

Questions regarding this handbook should be directed to the Human Resources Department.

Employees should always consult the latest approved version of this document.
